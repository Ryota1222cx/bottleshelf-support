Sakadana Support

Sakadana is an iPhone and Android app for adults of legal drinking age to keep a shelf of the bottles they own and to write tasting notes. Take a photo of a bottle's label and the app suggests what the bottle is.

FAQ

  • Bottle records: name, distillery, region, ABV, volume, purchase date, shop and price, opened or sealed, how much is left, and notes. Records stay on your device and are never sent to our servers.
  • Label recognition: the photo you take is shrunk and stripped of location data, then sent to our relay server to suggest bottle names. The relay server passes the image to Anthropic, PBC's AI (Claude) to read the printed text. After the suggestions come back, the photo is not kept on the relay server.
  • Tasting notes: date, rating, nose, palate, finish, how you drank it, and a photo. Also stored only on your device.
  • CSV export and import: export bottles and tastings to one CSV file that opens in spreadsheet apps. Photos are not included.
  • Deleting your data: Settings, then "Delete all data". Bottles, tastings, photos and settings are erased. Deleting the app also erases them.
  • Moving to a new phone: records live on the device, so move them with an iCloud or Google backup. Use "Restore purchases" to get your purchase back.

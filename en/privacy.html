Privacy Policy

Sakadana (the "App") is provided by Ryota Masuda (the "Operator"). This policy explains what the App handles, where it is sent, and how to delete it.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Bottle records: name, distillery, region, ABV, volume, purchase date, shop and price, opened state, amount left, notes.
  • Tasting notes: date, rating, nose, palate, finish, how you drank it, photo.
  • Photos: photos you take or pick are saved on the device. They are redrawn when saved, so location data is not kept.
  • Settings: language, the kinds of drinks you collect, an anonymous ID the App creates, and whether you finished the intro.

None of this is sent to the Operator. The only server the Operator runs is the label recognition relay below; there is no server that stores your records. If you use iCloud or Google backup, the operating system may include this data in your backup.

2. Data sent outside the device

  • Label recognition: only when you use it, the App sends a shrunk photo without location data, the anonymous ID, the kinds of drinks you collect, the language and the app version to the relay server. The relay server passes the image to Anthropic, PBC (USA) and its AI, Claude, to read the printed text. The photo is not kept on the relay server after the suggestions are returned.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): to manage purchases, the anonymous ID and purchase records (product, date). No email address or name.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (USA, Sentry): when the App stops unexpectedly, the error type, the screen name, the device model and OS version, so the bug can be fixed. Tap targets (which can contain bottle names or prices), your records and photos are not sent.
  • Apple and Google: purchases go through the App Store and Google Play.

The App shows no ads and does no tracking for advertising.

3. Purposes

To suggest bottle names from your photo, to keep your shelf and tasting notes, to manage purchases, and to fix bugs. Nothing else.

4. Deletion

Settings, then "Delete all data" erases the records, photos and settings on the device and creates a new anonymous ID. Deleting the App also erases them. RevenueCat purchase records are tied to Apple and Google purchase records and cannot be deleted from the App; email us if you want them removed.

5. Age

The App is for people of legal drinking age where they live. If you are younger, do not use it. The App asks you to confirm your age the first time you open it.

7. Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. If the App starts handling new data or sending it to new parties, it will also tell you inside the App.

1. What the App does

The App keeps a shelf of your bottles and your tasting notes. When you photograph a label, it suggests bottle names based only on the printed text. Suggestions are not guaranteed to be correct. If there is no suggestion or the photo is unclear, enter the bottle by hand.

2. Price

Up to 5 bottles and 2 tastings are free. Label recognition has a monthly limit, and the remaining count is shown on the recognition screen.

"Sakadana Pro" is a one-time purchase, not an auto-renewing subscription. It removes the bottle and tasting limits, adds CSV export and import, and raises the monthly recognition limit. The price is shown on the purchase screen. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, and refunds follow Apple's and Google's rules. Use "Restore purchases" on a new phone.
